## Top Land Management Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Permitting, Planning, Zoning, Inspections, Code Enforcement, Parcel & Community Development for Local Government*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Land Management** in the public sector. These systems help cities and counties manage land-use planning, building permits, inspections, licensing, code enforcement, and related community development workflows.

**Examples** include Accela Land Management, OpenGov Land Management, Tyler EnerGov, CityView, Clariti, CityWorks, Infor Public Sector, CentralSquare, ArcGIS Urban, and Cloudpermit (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Full municipal land management and permitting suites are almost entirely commercial. Practical open options exist mainly in **land recordation** (STDM), **landscape/wildfire planning** (Planscape), experimental **permitting portals**, and GIS-centric stacks (QGIS/PostGIS). This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Accela Land Management](https://www.accela.com/)**  
  Civic platform for land management, permitting, licensing, and related community development workflows used by many local governments.

- **[OpenGov Land Management / Permitting](https://opengov.com/)**  
  Modern cloud platform for permitting, licensing, and community development with a focus on citizen experience and agency efficiency.

- **[Tyler EnerGov](https://www.tylertech.com/)**  
  Enterprise community development suite supporting planning, permitting, licensing, inspections, and code enforcement, often paired with Tyler ERP.

- **[CityView](https://www.cityview.com/)**  
  Land management and community development software for permitting, planning, and related municipal processes.

- **[Clariti](https://www.clariti.com/)**  
  Cloud permitting, licensing, and inspections solution aimed at state and local governments.

- **[Cityworks (Trimble)](https://www.cityworks.com/)**  
  GIS-centric asset and work management platform frequently used alongside land and public-works workflows; strong Esri integration.

- **[Infor Public Sector](https://www.infor.com/)**  
  Public-sector ERP and community development capabilities including permitting and land-related processes.

- **[CentralSquare](https://www.centralsquare.com/)**  
  Community development and public administration software covering permitting, planning, and related local government functions.

- **[ArcGIS Urban (Esri)](https://www.esri.com/en-us/arcgis/products/arcgis-urban/overview)**  
  3D urban planning and scenario analysis platform for visualizing zoning, development capacity, and land-use plans.

- **[Cloudpermit](https://cloudpermit.com/)**  
  Cloud permitting and building control platform used by municipalities for digital permit applications and reviews.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[STDM – Social Tenure Domain Model](https://github.com/gltn/stdm)**  
  Free and open-source pro-poor land recordation system built on QGIS, PostgreSQL, and PostGIS for flexible people–land relationships.

- **[Planscape](https://github.com/OurPlanscape/Planscape)**  
  Open wildfire resilience and landscape planning application that combines spatial data and models to prioritize land treatments.

- **[OpenPermit / open regulatory infrastructure experiments](https://github.com/)**  
  Projects exploring open standards and reference implementations for permitting processes, evidence, and regulatory workflows.

- **[Federal and open permit portal prototypes](https://github.com/)**  
  Open-source permitting portal efforts aimed at streamlining environmental or multi-agency review (e.g., NEPA-oriented designs).

- **[QGIS + PostGIS land and parcel stacks](https://github.com/)**  
  Self-hosted geospatial stacks used by agencies and NGOs for cadastral mapping, zoning layers, and land analysis.

- **[Development permission and planning open systems](https://github.com/)**  
  Jurisdiction-specific open tools for development permission assessment and related spatial decision support.

- **[Citizen request and 311 open platforms](https://github.com/)**  
  Open systems that can complement land management by handling complaints, inspections requests, and service cases.

- **[Document and workflow open engines](https://github.com/)**  
  General open workflow and document management tools adaptable to permit routing and plan review.

- **[Parcel and cadastral open data tools](https://github.com/)**  
  Libraries and pipelines for ingesting, validating, and publishing parcel and land-record data.

- **[Public-sector open GIS reporting tools](https://github.com/)**  
  Open components for generating maps, notices, and reports tied to land and permit records.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Using **STDM** for flexible land tenure and recordation projects, especially in development or informal-settlement contexts.
- Adopting **Planscape**-style tools for landscape-scale planning and prioritization.
- Building GIS-centric land views on **QGIS + PostGIS** while keeping permitting in a commercial system.
- Accepting that full digital permitting, plan review, inspections, fee calculation, and citizen portals at municipal scale still require commercial platforms (Accela, Tyler EnerGov, OpenGov, Clariti, CentralSquare, Cloudpermit, etc.).
- Focusing open-source efforts on transparent land data, spatial analysis, and tenure security rather than replacing enterprise community development suites.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Maintain parcels and zoning in PostGIS/QGIS → run planning analyses with open spatial tools → handle formal permitting and inspections in a commercial land management system → publish selected data openly for transparency. Suitable for agencies with strong GIS capacity or for pilot/tenure projects. Most cities and counties continue to rely on commercial land management platforms for production permitting and compliance.
